## Signing Procedure

### For the Corporation

1. Contact the Project Owner (K-Arthur via GitHub) to initiate the
   corporate signing process.
2. The Project Owner will provide a copy of this Agreement for signature
   by an authorised officer of the Corporation.
3. Return the signed Agreement to the Project Owner. The Project Owner
   will confirm receipt and activate the corporate agreement.
4. Once active, the Corporation's authorised Contributors will be added to
   an allowlist or recognised by the CLA system.

### For Individual Contributors (Employees/Contractors)

On your first pull request after the Corporation has signed the CCLA:

1. The CLA Assistant bot will post a comment with a signing link.
2. Click the link and confirm that you are contributing on behalf of the
   Corporation.
3. The system will record your confirmation linked to the corporate
   agreement.
