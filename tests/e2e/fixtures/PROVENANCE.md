## Generative evidence

`generative-evidence/sd15-q4_0-landscape-replace/` contains the prepared source,
explicit source-pixel mask, raw model candidate, final masked composite,
difference map, and [run manifest](generative-evidence/sd15-q4_0-landscape-replace/manifest.json).
The run completed, but its inspected output is not acceptable
prompt-conditioned quality; it is retained as a failed qualification sample,
not as product proof.

`generative-evidence/qualification-2026-09-12/` contains the additional
diffusion-rs, llama-box, and upstream stable-diffusion.cpp candidates inspected
on the same real photograph. The [manifest](generative-evidence/qualification-2026-09-12/manifest.json)
records the exact model/runtime, settings, output hashes, and failure reason.
No candidate in that run is release-qualified.

`generative-evidence/diagnostic-2026-09-14-native-helper/` retains the mask and
the two post-patch production-helper outputs from the SD 2 F16 and SD 1.5 Q4_0
real-photo probe. Both are rejected diagnostic candidates; the exact settings,
hashes, and review decision are recorded in the [native boundary audit](../../../docs/audits/generative-editing-native-probe-2026-09-14.md).

`generative-evidence/expand-2026-09-13/` also retains the reviewed browser
Expand dialog and the independently decoded 1664x1272 PNG export. The
`subtract-2026-09-13/` directory retains the corresponding Remove /
Generative Subtract dialog and applied-scene captures from the real-photo
Chromium workflow; each directory has a hash manifest. These are workflow
captures, not model-quality claims: the browser lanes use the local Fast /
PatchMatch provider, while native LaMa quality is qualified separately.

Project-generated images remain marked above. The 24 `real-life-*.jpg`
fixtures were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons from files whose source pages
record public-domain, CC0, or CC BY-SA terms. Thumbnail transformations are
recorded per row and in the frozen manifest; the SHA-256 values identify the
exact bytes committed here. They are included only as local test inputs so
visual and image-workflow checks exercise photographic content offline. The
corpus contains 24 photographs and 32 frozen tasks; model qualification remains
a separate evidence step.
